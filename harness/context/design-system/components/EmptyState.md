# EmptyState

What fills a screen or panel that has nothing in it yet: an empty library, no bookmarks, no search matches.

- An icon in an `accent-soft` disc, a `title-lg` heading, one or two sentences of `body` in `ink-muted`, and at most one action.
- Say what will appear here and how to get it: "Highlight a passage and it will appear here." Not "Nothing here!".
- In panels, drop the action and keep it short.

Consumer provides: `title`, optional `icon`, `children` (the sentence), `action`.
